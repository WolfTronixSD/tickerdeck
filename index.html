TickerDeck Live

Real-time stock prices that stream in while the app is open. It runs on a free market-data key from Finnhub, and the key stays on this phone.

  1. Open finnhub.io/register and create a free account.
  2. Copy the API key shown on your Finnhub dashboard.
  3. Paste it below.

Tip: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and TickerDeck opens full screen like an app.
